# Project 03: Customer Churn / Sales Trend Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analysing e-commerce sales data to identify revenue trends, top-performing product categories, and valuable customers. The goal is to generate useful business insights and recommendations through data analysis and visualization.

## Dataset Information
- **Data type:** Generated synthetic e-commerce sales data
- **Total orders:** 1,500
- **Unique customers:** 168
- **Product categories:** 5
- **Date range:** January–December 2025

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Analysis Performed
1. Data cleaning and preprocessing
2. Feature engineering and date extraction
3. Monthly revenue trend analysis
4. Product category performance analysis
5. Monthly category comparison using pivot tables
6. Customer spending analysis
7. Identification of high-value customers
8. Business recommendations

## Key Findings
- **Highest revenue month:** May – 622,477.89
- **Lowest revenue month:** February – 396,928.10
- **Top-performing category:** Electronics – 2,399,899.92
- **Lowest-performing category:** Beauty – 597,797.61
- **Top 10% of customers:** Contributed approximately 64.98% of total revenue.

## Business Recommendations
- Introduce loyalty rewards for high-value customers.
- Focus on inventory planning and marketing for Electronics.
- Improve Beauty category performance through targeted promotions.
- Investigate monthly revenue differences to plan better sales campaigns.
