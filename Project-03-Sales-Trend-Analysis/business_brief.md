# Business Brief: E-commerce Sales Trend Analysis

## 1. Business Objective
The objective of this analysis is to understand e-commerce sales performance, identify top-performing product categories, study monthly revenue trends, and recognize high-value customers.

## 2. Dataset Overview
- Total orders: 1,500
- Unique customers: 168
- Product categories: 5
- Analysis period: January–December 2025
- Data source: Generated synthetic e-commerce dataset

## 3. Key Findings

### Revenue Trends
- May recorded the highest monthly revenue of 622,477.89.
- February recorded the lowest monthly revenue of 396,928.10.
- Monthly revenue analysis helps identify periods of stronger and weaker sales.

### Product Category Performance
- Electronics generated the highest revenue of 2,399,899.92.
- Beauty generated the lowest revenue of 597,797.61.
- Electronics contributed approximately 38.32% of total revenue.

### Customer Analysis
- The top 10% of customers (17 customers) contributed approximately 64.98% of total business revenue.
- This indicates that a relatively small group of customers accounts for a large share of revenue.

## 4. Business Recommendations

1. **Customer Retention:** Introduce loyalty programmes, exclusive offers, and personalized rewards for high-value customers.

2. **Category Improvement:** Prioritize Electronics inventory and marketing while exploring promotions and customer preferences to improve Beauty category sales.

3. **Sales Planning:** Investigate why May performed better than February. Use seasonal demand, promotions, and inventory information to plan future campaigns.

4. **Customer Engagement:** Monitor customer purchase patterns and develop targeted marketing strategies to encourage repeat purchases.

## 5. Conclusion
The analysis highlights important revenue patterns, category performance differences, and the contribution of high-value customers. These insights can help businesses make better decisions about marketing, inventory management, and customer retention.

## 6. Data Disclaimer
This analysis uses generated synthetic data for educational purposes. It does not contain real customer or business transaction records.
